What is BizLeap?

Bizleap is a premier business networking and growth platform where structured development is achieved through qualified training, workshops, and high-impact events.

Membership is restricted to Corporates, Companies, Firms, and Professionals only. An individual person is not eligible for membership; every member must represent an entity as an authorised top-level person (Proprietor, Director, or Partner).

Founded by Aayush Lohia, BizLeap provides 100% category exclusivity, ensuring a competitive-free environment for members to develop mutual relationships based on trust and knowledge.

Our Mission

To help members increase their business through a structured, positive referral marketing program that enables them to develop long-term, meaningful relationships.

Our Vision

To engage entrepreneurs in a mood of goodness, performing business activities as a service to the divine, turning material growth into spiritually guided progress.

Our Objective

To provide a structured platform where businesses achieve growth through verified Peer-to-Peer meetings, qualified training, and global networking initiatives.
