Ethics & Code of Conduct

Business Dress Code

Dressing in Business Formals is mandatory for all official meetings. Casual/Semi-formal attire is not permitted.

  • MEN: Modi/Nehru Jacket OR Blazer with Tie. (No Jeans allowed).
  • WOMEN: Business Formals (Indian or Western). No short/mini clothing allowed.

Member Guidelines

  • Decorum: Maintain high values of language ensuring mutual respect within and outside meetings.
  • Genuine Connections: Pass Biz Connections with a high intent of ensuring fellow member's growth.
  • Relationships: Build long-term mutual business relationships with no short-term motives or ill-intent.
  • Grievances: Approach the Redressal Committee or Chapter Councillor. Avoid loose talk or cross-talk with other members.
