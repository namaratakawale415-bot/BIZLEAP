Mood of EQUIPOISE

Bizleap believes that all stipulated responsibilities are to be performed in the mood of EQUIPOISE—an even state of mind without attachment to success or failure, completely detached to results of actions which are directly linked to ecstasy or sorrow.

As per BG 2.47-2.48, we believe that executing our prescribed actions is in our hands but not in our control, and neither do we control the outcome of such actions. All acts should be performed in a mood of goodness, spiritually guided, and detached from fruitive results.
